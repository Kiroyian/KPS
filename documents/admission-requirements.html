Karero Primary School Admission Requirements

Please attach or present the following items when submitting the completed application form:

  • Completed admission application form.
  • Copy of the learner's birth certificate.
  • Copy of parent or guardian national ID.
  • Previous school report or transfer letter, where applicable.
  • Two recent passport-size photos of the learner.
  • Any relevant medical information the school should know.
Submission: Completed forms should be submitted to the school office for review.
